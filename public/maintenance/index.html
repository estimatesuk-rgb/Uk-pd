How the pricing works

V8 is the agreed selling rate. At 0%, the total is exactly quantity × V8 rate. Adjustment ±% is applied to V8, and its labour/material portions, only once. SMV minutes × quantity / 60 gives labour hours.

Subcontractor Labour-Only & Signed Timesheets

Prepare separate labour-only work schedules, record completed quantities and actual hours, then print a site timesheet for subcontractor and supervisor signatures.

Rate separation: Your V8 labour allocation is only an editable reference, not an agreed subcontract price. No client V8 selling-rate percentage or OH&P adjustment is added. Check and confirm the subcontractor's own labour-only rate before payment. Signing is done on the printed/PDF sheet; this is not an electronic-signature service.

1. Agree labour-only subcontract rates

Enter an agreed £/unit for measured works, or switch an activity to £/hour and specify the agreed hourly rate. Tick Agreed? individually to confirm the rate; changing it automatically removes that confirmation.
