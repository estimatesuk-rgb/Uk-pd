Office follow-up reminder settings

Three weekly quotation chases

Each pending quotation marked as issued can generate reminders 1, 2 and 3 at roughly days 7, 14 and 21. The automated emails go to enquiries@ukestimates.com only, and each reminder links to its Job File. The sequence stops if the client accepts, declines or all three reminders have been sent.

Email delivery status: Checking…

Cloudflare schedule: weekdays at 09:00 UTC (10:00 during British Summer Time); jobs falling due at weekends are reminded on the next weekday.

Staff can send a client chase email from the Job File with the practice office BCCed, and the message is recorded in the job timeline. If they email externally, they must confirm the chase and upload the sent .eml/.msg copy themselves.

To activate automatic sending, Cloudflare requires the Resend RESEND_API_KEY secret and a verified MAIL_FROM address. Never enter your email API key into this website or send it in chat.

Practice protection

Encrypted practice backup

Export saved jobs, fee proposals, timeline notes, correspondence, documents and practice settings without passwords or login sessions. Your download is encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM.

Cloudflare's SQLite Durable Object also includes rolling 30-day point-in-time recovery. Download and store backups off-site regularly; this download is not an automatic off-site copy.
