01 / Project brief

Start with the space.

Enter the proposed floor area. Build cost, architects' fees and VAT update automatically.

Site / project address
Construction cost defaults to £2,500/m² before VAT. This is an early budgeting allowance, not a measured tender or a guaranteed build price. VAT treatment, particularly on new dwellings, must be checked for the actual works.
02 / Design service

Choose the appointment.

Select or omit stages and edit the £/m² fee. Only where the actual area is less than 25 m², each selected stage is charged as 25 m². Building Regulations at £55/m² is £1,375; every other stage is pro rata at its own rate. At 25 m² or over, there is no minimum. Fixed-fee extras remain separate.

03 / Additions

Optional services.

These are indicative practice allowances, editable for each project. Tick only services you are proposing to deliver.

04 / Variations

Additions & omissions.

Add project-specific extra fees or deductions. Omissions reduce the architects' fee and appear separately in the proposal.

05 / Fee exclusions

What's not included.

Tick exclusions to print on the fee proposal. Unselected architectural stages are also identified automatically as omissions.

06 / Practice & saved projects

Finalise your proposal.

Start typing a UK street/place or postcode for online suggestions. The free lookups use OpenStreetMap/Photon for street and place matches and Postcodes.io for postcodes.
