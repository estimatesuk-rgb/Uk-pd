# Complete RAMS / Principal Designer output standard

## Reference and privacy
Source: user-supplied 94-page Rev D Construction Phase Plan, Risk Assessments & Method Statements Word exemplar (October 2026). This repository stores **only the abstract output specification**, never the original document, personal data, project value, client identity or other project-specific particulars. The exemplar is a formatting and coverage benchmark, **not** an automatic legal compliance certificate.

## Mandatory report appearance
- Formal A4 portrait report with the producing organisation's approved logo on every page, centre-weighted front title, dark navy table heading bands, red section headings, consistent controlled-document footer, and page numbering.
- First page: project title, client, full site address, principal contractor, document references and revisions, drawing references, document revision/date, and controlled-document notice. Principal Designer and Principal Contractor branded documents must use the appropriate selected legal entity; do not silently swap dutyholders or suggest a PD approved the contractor's task RAMS.
- Revision-control table and dutyholder/appointment table, including named and evidenced PC, PD, client, site manager, temporary works coordinator, lifting appointed person, first aid, fire marshal and environmental lead when relevant.
- Separate numbered, fully populated risk assessment sheets and separate numbered, linked method statement sheets; do **not** merge both into a brief generic table.
- Preserve table-based layout, signature and reviewer spaces, individual references, author, approver, acceptance status, dates and revision trace.
- Offer downloadable editable Word (.docx) and printable PDF; identical document contents and controlled issue states across formats. Printed browser HTML can be an additional preview, not the sole export.
- Section count is determined by project scope; the source's 94 pages and its 28+24+12 entries describe a reference example, not mandatory irrelevant construction work for every project.

## Master Construction Phase Plan sections
1. Document control and purpose; site-specific scope and occupied/operational interfaces; revisions and written appointments.
2. Applicable legal/management framework, CDM 2015 duties, Schedule 3 particular risks, F10 screening and notifications when triggered.
3. Site establishment, security, boundaries, segregation, visitors and traffic management.
4. Interfaces with ongoing operations, service interruptions, neighbours/public and emergency access.
5. Schedule 2 welfare provisions, responsible people and maintenance checks.
6. Competence, induction, workforce consultation, toolbox talks and coordination meetings.
7. Task permits, temporary works checks, lifting/isolations and pre-start hold points.
8. Fire, first aid, evacuation, emergency services directions and rescue arrangements.
9. Health controls: silica/RCS, substances, dermatitis, noise, vibration, manual handling and surveillance where required.
10. Waste and environmental controls, spill response, ecological constraints.
11. Risk-matrix legend, assessor-selected initial/residual LxS ratings and stop-work/action thresholds.

## Each individual Risk Assessment (RA)
- Unique RA number, work activity, project, exact location, applicability and assessment status.
- People at risk and review triggers.
- One row per specific hazard: hazard, who may be harmed, initial likelihood x severity, detailed hierarchical control measures, post-control likelihood x severity.
- Hold points/records: competency, inspection certificates, manufacturer instructions, subcontractor RAMS acceptance, permits, design information, stop-work triggers.
- Assessor name/date, supervisor approval/acceptance, reviewer, next review, source drawings and evidence.
- Scores are explicitly **assessor-confirmed**; never default all items to the same residual score, infer 'safe' status from presence of text or issue a blanket approval.

## Each individual Method Statement (MS)
- Independent MS number, precise package, location and cross-reference to the *correct* RA ID.
- Scope, operative roles, qualifications and supervision.
- Task-specific plant/access/PPE/RPE, design inputs and current site limitations.
- Detailed numbered sequence in execution order.
- Mandatory controls, inspection releases, permits and hold points.
- Emergency, rescue and environmental measures.
- Separate worker briefing table (name, employer/trade if appropriate, supervisor, date, signature).
- Validate RA↔MS relationship against the activity text; the reference exemplar contains cross-references that must be reviewed rather than reproduced uncritically.

## COSHH, substance screening and detailed records
- Substance register and individual assessments, including: Portland cement/wet concrete; RCS cutting dust; diesel; hydraulic oil; PU foam/isocyanates; sealants; coatings; welding fumes; compressed gas cylinders; cleaning chemicals; bituminous products; landscaping chemicals where relevant.
- Each record: exact product/current SDS, hazard, route/duration/quantity of exposure, people affected, substitution/engineering controls, PPE/RPE and face fit where appropriate, handling/storage/disposal, first aid/fire/spill, exposure monitoring/health surveillance if required, competent assessment and review date.
- A master entry is not a substitute for the actual manufacturer/product SDS or task exposure assessment. Unconfirmed details remain flagged.

## Forms and registers required by the exemplar
1. Permit to dig: locations, service plans, CAT/Genny, trial holes, permitted plant/depth, authorisation and close-out.
2. Hot-work permit: workface, combustibles, extinguishers, gas cylinders, fire watches, validity and close-out.
3. Roof/work-at-height permit: access/edge protection, fragile areas, weather, anchor/harness, rescue, exclusion and authorisation.
4. Daily Site Manager checks: security, welfare, traffic, excavations, scaffold/MEWP, lifting, housekeeping, weather, permits, occupied interface.
5. Weekly Principal Contractor inspection: management, fire, welfare, traffic, services, structural stability, work at height, LOLER, COSHH/health, electrical, environmental and action closeout.
6. Toolbox-talk/RAMS briefing with signatures.
7. Incident/near-miss initial record with RIDDOR screening.
8. Temporary works register with designer, checker, approval and release status.
9. Health and Safety File handover index: as-built information, residual risks, services, roof/fall protection, specialist installations, fire, O&M and tests/certifications.
10. Final pre-start/package-release checklist: no unconfirmed critical information or missing required approval.
11. Approval and acceptance signatures: correct PC authority, site manager, PD acknowledgment and client acknowledgment where appropriate.

## Drawings-to-RAMS traceability
- Analyse uploaded drawings, notes, specifications, programmes and PCI by document name, revision, sheet/page and location.
- Present candidate work activities from those inputs with confidence and allow the user to accept/reject/modify and record a reason.
- Explicitly ask construction method, materials, plant, sequence, suppliers, working heights, excavations, lifting loads, temporary works, services, interfaces and hazardous substances whenever these cannot be established.
- Generate only applicable activities, with recorded reasons for omissions, and add missing-information/RFI list.
- Link each RA, MS, COSHH and permit to drawings/PCI/decisions, and invalidate/review affected items on source revision.
- Resolve missing SDS, engineer designs, lifting plans, rescue plans, programme, competency/permit evidence and task-specific subcontractor procedures before issue.
- Permit drafts with prominent **DRAFT / NOT APPROVED** marks, but do not claim professional review, statutory approval or site readiness without documented authorized acceptance.

## Principal Designer-specific companion output
- Identify the PD's design-phase duties distinctly from the PC's CPP and contractor work sequencing duties.
- Include design-risk register, elimination/reduction decisions and residual significant risks with drawing links, coordination log, PCI completeness/action schedule, Schedule 3 work identification, constructability/maintenance interfaces, changes and handover/H&S File inputs.
- Never claim that a PD-generated draft has approved contractor RAMS; use correct role-specific signature/acknowledgment wording.

## Release acceptance tests
- A whole project produces separate, individually numbered CPP/RA/MS/COSHH/permit/register pages that are recognisably formatted like the reference's first page, example RA page and example MS page.
- All applicable sections have substantive fields and linked evidence; no template filler is silently presented as completed.
- Work requiring specialist method, permits, review or inspection is marked blocked until satisfied.
- Full pack exports accurately to Word and PDF with logo, headers, footers, page breaks, tables and signatures.
- Non-applicable topics are excluded only after a scope decision with justification and preserved audit trail.
- Test with a real uploaded plan set and review by a competent construction professional before production release.
